Creator Platform Agreement

This agreement is between {{ platform_name }} ("the Platform") and {{ legal_name }} ({{ email }}), Instagram @{{ ig_username }} ("the Creator"), dated {{ today }}.

1. What the Platform does

The Platform sells content and promotion services to brands and engages creators to deliver them. For each campaign, the Platform offers the Creator a fixed fee ("Creator Fee") for defined deliverables. The Creator Fee is payable by the Platform. The Platform sets its own price to brands, and that price is not part of this agreement.

2. Campaign offers

Each offer shows the brief, deliverables, Creator Fee, timeline, usage rights, exclusivity and minimum live period. The Creator may accept or decline any offer. Accepting an offer creates a separate Campaign Agreement, signed electronically, which governs that campaign.

3. Content, approval and publishing

  • Nothing is published on the Creator's account without the Creator's approval of the exact final content and caption.
  • If the Creator has connected Instagram publishing, the Platform may publish only content the Creator approved, at the approved time.
  • Every sponsored post carries a clear paid-partnership disclosure (for example "#ad" and the paid partnership label), as required by ASCI guidelines. The Creator must not remove it.
  • AI may help with scripts, captions and editing. AI-generated content using the Creator's face or voice is only produced if the Creator has opted in, and each such asset still needs the Creator's explicit approval.

4. Posting period and payment

  • After publishing, the Platform checks the post daily for {{ verification_days }} days. When the post has stayed live and unedited for that period, the Creator Fee is paid to the Creator's verified bank account, minus TDS as required by law.
  • The post must remain live for at least {{ min_live_days }} days, or longer if stated in the Campaign Agreement. If it is removed or the disclosure is edited out early, the Platform may withhold or recover the Creator Fee for that campaign.
  • Creators registered for GST will invoice the Platform with GST; the Platform provides TDS certificates.

5. Usage rights

For each campaign, the Creator grants the brand and the Platform the rights stated in the Campaign Agreement. This normally includes reposting on the brand's channels and use in paid social ads for the stated period. The Creator may be asked to grant the brand partner access in the Instagram app to run those ads.

6. Creator commitments

  • Information in the profile, including audience statistics, is accurate. The Creator does not use bought followers, likes or engagement groups.
  • The Creator makes only the product claims in the confirmed brief and follows applicable advertising law.
  • For 12 months after being introduced to a brand through the Platform, the Creator will not accept paid work from that brand outside the Platform.

7. Data

The Platform processes the Creator's personal data, KYC details and Instagram data to run the service, match campaigns, pay the Creator and meet legal obligations, as described in the privacy notice. Aggregated audience data and campaign post performance are shared with brands for campaigns the Creator joins. KYC and bank details are never shared with brands.

8. Term and exit

Either party may end this agreement with notice in the app. Campaigns already accepted continue under their Campaign Agreements. The Creator may request deletion of their data, subject to legal record-keeping duties.

9. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. It is signed electronically under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
