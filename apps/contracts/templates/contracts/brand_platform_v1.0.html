Brand Platform Agreement

This agreement is between {{ platform_name }} ("the Platform") and {{ company_name }}{% if gstin %} (GSTIN {{ gstin }}){% endif %}, represented by {{ signer_email }} ("the Brand"), dated {{ today }}.

1. Service

The Platform provides creator marketing services: understanding the Brand's product, recommending creators, producing and publishing sponsored content through creators it engages, and reporting results. The Platform is the Brand's sole counterparty; creators are engaged by the Platform.

2. Campaign orders and price

  • Each campaign is a separate order showing the selected creators, deliverables and campaign price. The Brand pays the campaign price plus GST before production starts. Prices are fixed per order.
  • If a selected creator declines, misses a deadline or is replaced, the Platform offers a comparable replacement or refunds that part of the order.

3. Brief and claims

The Brand confirms the product brief before creators are contacted and is responsible for the accuracy of product information and any claims in it. Restricted categories (such as alcohol, financial products, health products) need extra documentation and may be declined.

4. Content review

The Brand may request up to two rounds of revisions per deliverable. If the Brand doesn't respond within {{ brand_review_hours }} hours of content being submitted, the content is treated as approved. The creator must also approve the final content before it is published.

5. Usage rights

Unless the order says otherwise, the Brand may repost campaign content on its own channels and use it in paid social ads for {{ usage_rights_days }} days from publishing. Extensions can be bought through the Platform.

6. Disclosure and conduct

All sponsored content carries a paid-partnership disclosure, which the Brand must not ask creators to remove. The Brand will not contact creators introduced through the Platform to arrange paid work outside the Platform for 12 months.

7. Results

Reach, engagement and sales estimates are predictions, not guarantees.

8. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. It is signed electronically under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
